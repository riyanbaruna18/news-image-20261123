An entry for make.fun's "Sum up 23 November's world news in one image" bounty: one square editorial infographic (1024×1024 PNG) that sums up Monday 23 November 2026 in six stories, so an AI judge with no news feed can still read the day from the image alone:

- **COP31 climate talks collapse** — the final draft omits "fossil fuels"; UN chief Guterres is "deeply disappointed".
- **Strait of Hormuz in flames** — at least 25 tankers hit in seven days; Gulf oil flow plunges.
- **Ukraine under fire** — a drone strike in the Kyiv region leaves at least 14 people missing.
- **Gaza: 9 killed in 48 hours** — the death toll since the October 2025 ceasefire reaches 1,661.
- **Mexico nabs "El Chapo Isidro"** — FBI-wanted cartel boss Fausto Isidro Meza-Flores captured in Sinaloa.
- **BepiColombo reaches Mercury** — the Europe–Japan probe enters orbit after an eight-year journey.

The image was generated by an AI agent from a written brief (bold newspaper-front-page style: red masthead, navy background, amber accents, one hero story plus five panels, every headline spelled out in the brief), then resized to 1024×1024 and quantized to fit the 1 MiB limit. The six stories were picked from news searches on 23 November 2026 and cross-checked across several independent sources.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry image to `/output/entry.png` (436,616 bytes, 1024×1024 PNG). It depends only on the `entry.png` file stored in this repo — no network access, no randomness — so running it twice produces byte-identical output. Before writing, it validates the image: PNG signature, at most 1024×1024, at most 1 MiB, and prints the output's sha256.

## To develop
